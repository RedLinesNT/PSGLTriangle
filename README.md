This project is only intended to be a "<i>trace</i>" of myself learning something.
I wanted to get out of my "comfort place" (<i>desktop-based systems</i>) and discover new stuff, and the PlayStation(R)3 was for me a funny thing to try. Learning its constraints, workspace, tools, ...<br/>
What I made is an extremely primitive approach to <i>PSGL</i> and the <i>PlayStation(R)3</i> in itself. So there's no usage of the CELL's SPUs. (<i>I'll probably do that when I'll get my hands into GCM, you know, for fun</i>)

My goal was only to be able to render a triangle on a screen within the <i>PlayStation(R)3</i> to apply my freshly learned "<i>skills</i>" 
on the subject and NOT to develop an entire application/game. You can see this project as my determination to learn whatever and everything, if you want. :)<br/>
I'm a newbie when it comes to consoles, their constraints and the rest about them. I only have some theory.

What I've programmed is an extremely primitive approach to <i>PSGL</i> and the <i>PlayStation(R)3</i> in itself.
It could use a lot of improvement with a little more learning on my part.

<strong>I'm not able to provide the <i>SDKs</i>, <i>toolchains</i> or <i>compilers</i> used.</strong>
But to build the application yourself, you'll need the <i>PlayStation(R)3 SDK 4.75</i>, and an IDE supported by the SDK, such as <i>Visual Studio 2013 Community/ULTIMATE</i> or an older one.

The application itself is very, very simple. A grey background, a triangle spinning in the center of the screen and a color assigned to each Vertex that blends in between (the starting point of everything I guess).<br/>
Using a DualShock3/SIXAXIS controller, you can press:
<br/>
<table>
    <tr><td>CROSS</td><td>Enable VSync</td></tr>
    <tr><td>CIRCLE</td><td>Disable VSync</td></tr>
    <tr><td>DPAD UP</td><td>Move the camera forward</td></tr>
    <tr><td>DPAD DOWN</td><td>Move the camera backward</td></tr>
    <tr><td>DPAD LEFT</td><td>Rotate the camera toward the left</td></tr>
    <tr><td>DPAD RIGHT</td><td>Rotate the camera toward the right.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>R1</td><td>Increase the FOV</td></tr>
    <tr><td>L1</td><td>Decrease the FOV</td></tr>
</table>
